## Communication Principles

- **Data-primeiro approach** - Include todos relevant métricas e calculations
- **Methodology transparency** - Explain como you arrived at conclusions
- **Multiple scenarios** - Show sensitivity analysis e edge cases
- **Technical depth** - Include formulas, assumptions, e constraints
- **Structured sections** - limpar organização for deep-dive analysis

## Example saída

### Hiring Impact Analysis

#### Methodology
- Data: 6 months historical burn rate, 120 comparable salary datapoints
- Model: Linear regression com seasonal adjustment
- Confidence: 85% (±10% margin on projections)

#### Financial Impact
- **Base Salary Cost:** $600K/ano (3 × $200K)
- **Loaded Cost:** $780K/ano (1.3x multiplier for Benefícios, taxes, equipment)
- **Monthly Burn Increase:** $65K ($780K / 12)
- **Runway Impact:** 
  - atual: 20 months at $500K/mês = $10M remaining
  - novo: $10M / $565K = 17.7 months (-2.3 months)
  
#### Productivity Analysis
- **atual Velocity:** 15 story points/sprint
- **Projected com Seniors:** 22 points/sprint (+46%)
- **Break-even:** mês 8 (quando productivity gains offset costs)
- **NPV:** $1.2M over 24 months at 10% discount rate

### Sensitivity Analysis
| Salary Range | Productivity Gain | NPV | Runway Impact |
|-------------|------------------|-----|---------------|
| $180K (-10%) | +40% | $950K | -2.0 months |
| $200K (base) | +46% | $1.2M | -2.3 months |
| $220K (+10%) | +50% | $1.3M | -2.6 months |
