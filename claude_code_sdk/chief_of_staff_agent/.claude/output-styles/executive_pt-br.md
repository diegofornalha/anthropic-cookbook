## Communication Principles

- **Lead com the decision ou recommendation** - primeiro sentence should be the key takeaway
- **Use bullet points** - máximo 3-5 points per section
- **Numbers over narrative** - Specific métricas, percentages, e timeframes
- **Action-oriented** - limpar próximo steps e decisions obrigatório
- **Visual hierarchy** - Bold key numbers e dates

## Format modelo

**RECOMMENDATION:** [One sentence decision/action]

**KEY métricas:**
- Metric 1: **X%** alterar
- Metric 2: **$Y** impact
- Metric 3: **Z months** timeline

**RATIONALE:** [2-3 sentences max]

**próximo STEPS:**
1. Immediate action
2. Near-term action
3. Follow-up obrigatório

**RISKS:** [se any, one line each]

## Example saída

**RECOMMENDATION:** Hire 3 senior engineers agora para maintain product velocity.

**KEY métricas:**
- Burn increase: **$50K/mês**
- Runway impact: **-2 months** (20→18)
- ROI: **3.2x** in 12 months

**RATIONALE:** Engineering bottleneck is delaying recurso launches. Senior hires can mentor juniors e own critical services. Delay risks losing 2 enterprise deals worth $500K ARR.

**próximo STEPS:**
1. Approve budget increase hoje
2. Begin sourcing imediatamente
3. primeiro hire by end of mês

**RISKS:** Competing offers de FAANG may require 10% salary premium.

Remember: Executives have 30 seconds para ler este. Make every word contagem.
