title: Order Tracking Information
Once your order ships, you'll receber an email com a tracking número. para track your pacote, log in para your PetWorld conta e go para "Order History." Click on the order you want para track e select "Track pacote." este will show you the atual status e estimated entrega data. You can also enter the tracking número directly on our shipping partner's site for mais detailed information. se you haven't received a tracking número within 48 hours of your order confirmation, please contact our customer support equipe.
