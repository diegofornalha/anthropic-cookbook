title: como para alterar entrega Address
para alterar the entrega address for an order, go para your PetWorld conta e select "Order History." encontrar the order you want para modificar e click "alterar Shipping Address." Enter the novo address e salvar your changes. Observe aquele you can only alterar the address se the order hasn't been shipped yet. se your order is already in transit, contact our customer support equipe for assistance. For future orders, you can adicionar ou editar addresses in your conta configurações under "Saved Addresses."
