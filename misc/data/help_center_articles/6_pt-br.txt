title: Shipping opções e Policies
PetWorld offers several shipping opções para meet your needs. Standard shipping typically takes 3-5 business days e is free for orders over $49. Expedited shipping (2-3 business days) e overnight shipping are available for an additional fee. We atualmente ship para todos 50 U.S. states e select internacional destinations. Observe aquele alguns oversized items ou orders containing certain restricted products may have special shipping Requisitos. You can view todos available shipping opções e costs durante the checkout processo.
