SUBLEASE AGREEMENT

este SUBLEASE AGREEMENT (“Sublease”) is made e entered into on the 23 dia of April, by e entre Workrise Technologies Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Sublessor” ou “Sublandlord”), e CS Disco, Inc. a Delaware corporation (“Sublessee” ou “Subtenant”).

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Sublandlord e Cousins – One Congress Plaza LLC (“Landlord” ou “Master Landlord”) entered in a lease (“Lease”) on August 20, 2018.

WHEREAS, The Lease has been amended by aquele certain primeiro Amendment dated December 5, 2018 (the “primeiro Amendment”), by aquele certain segundo Amendment dated June 21, 2019 (the “segundo Amendment”), e by aquele certain Third Amendment dated June 21, 2019 (the “Third Amendment”), hereinafter collectively referred para as the “Master Lease” ou “Lease”, e as attached hereto as Exhibit “A”.

WHEREAS, Pursuant para the Lease, Landlord has leased para Tenant space atualmente containing aproximadamente 23,108 square feet of Rentable Floor Area (the “6th Floor Space”) (being Suite número 600) located on the entire 6th floor of the Building commonly known as One Eleven located at 111 Congress Avenue, Austin Texas 78701.

WHEREAS, Pursuant para the Lease, Landlord has leased para Tenant space atualmente containing aproximadamente 23,142 square feet of Rentable Floor Area (the “9th Floor Space”) (being Suite número 900) located on the entire 9th floor of the Building commonly known as One Eleven located at 111 Congress Avenue, Austin Texas 78701.

WHEREAS, Sublessee desires para sublet de Sublessor, e Sublessor has agreed para sublet para Sublessee, a portion of the Premises included in the Lease, specifically the 6th Floor Space e the 9th Floor Space (the “Subleased Premises”), upon the terms e conditions set forth in este Sublease.

agora THEREFORE, for good e valuable consideration e on the terms e upon the conditions set forth herein, Sublessor e Sublessee hereby agree as follows:
